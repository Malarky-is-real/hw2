What Drives Me

The main thing driving me to work hard everday is the feeling of being someone's saviour. Not in the typical prince charming way, but in the

"Anthony is here, so I know things will be okay." This stems from a helplessness I felt as a child, when things would happen that seemed almost impossible to deal with. I never wanted anyone else to feel this way and as such, take joy in roles where I help others. Mainly customer service.
